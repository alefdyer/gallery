2. What this feature does

Gallery can show photos and videos from a remote server that you configure (WebDAV or Nextcloud). The app connects directly from your device to the server address you enter, using the login and password (or app password) you provide.

3. Your credentials and local encryption

  • The server URL and login are stored in the app’s local database on your device for display and connection.
  • The password is stored only on this device, in encrypted form (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences / Keystore). It is used solely to authenticate to your WebDAV/Nextcloud server.
  • Asinosoft does not receive, collect, or relay your WebDAV/Nextcloud password to Asinosoft servers. The password is not uploaded to asinosoft.ru or any Asinosoft backend.
  • Cloud backup of the app may exclude these encrypted credentials; after restore on another device you may need to sign in again.

4. Your server, your responsibility

  • You are responsible for the WebDAV/Nextcloud (or other compatible) host you choose, including its security, TLS configuration, access rights, and privacy policy.
  • We do not operate your server. Media files stay with that host under its terms.
  • Use a strong password or Nextcloud app password; prefer HTTPS.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your WebDAV/Nextcloud credentials.
  • We do not require an Asinosoft account to use WebDAV/Nextcloud in Gallery.
  • Connecting the storage is optional; you can delete it anytime in Network settings.
